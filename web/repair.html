      What a result does—and does not—prove

      Passed: the required invariants held for the declared local inputs and finite schedule. Violation: observed effects or completion behavior broke the contract. Incomplete: required execution or observations are missing. Unresolved: the effect is unsupported or observations conflict.

      The receipt is the entire simulated fulfillment effect. This does not certify an external payment, email or shipment, prove exactly-once SQS delivery, or test every interleaving. The local fault interrupts the call after receipt commit and before its response; the archived AWS worker crashed after receiving success.

      This browser inspects reports. It does not execute Python, call AWS or authenticate report origin. No live cloud experiment can be started from this page.
